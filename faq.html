Redemption Questions & Troubleshooting

What are Dead by Daylight promo codes?

Dead by Daylight promo codes are official promotional codes issued by Behaviour Interactive that grant players in-game rewards including Bloodpoints, Auric Cells, Charms, and cosmetic outfits. They are released during streams, patch launches, and community milestones.

How do I redeem a code in Dead by Daylight?

To redeem a promo code in Dead by Daylight:

  1. Launch Dead by Daylight on PC, PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo Switch.
  2. Click the Store button at the bottom right corner of the main lobby.
  3. Click the Redeem Code button located in the top-right corner of the store screen.
  4. Enter your promo code exactly as displayed and click Redeem.

Why did my Dead by Daylight promo code fail or say invalid?

Codes can fail if they have expired, reached a global server limit, were entered with typographical errors, or have already been redeemed on that specific player account. Double-check your spelling and ensure capital letters match.

Do Dead by Daylight Bloodpoint codes expire?

Yes, event and livestream promo codes often expire after 24 to 72 hours, whereas milestone reward codes may remain active for several months. DBD Codes verifies all codes daily so only working rewards are listed.

Are DBD promo codes safe to use on console and PC?

Yes, 100%. All codes listed on DBD Codes are redeemed directly within the official game client through Behaviour Interactive's in-game store. No third-party software or account modifications are required.

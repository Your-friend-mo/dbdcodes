Privacy Policy

How DBD Codes respects your privacy, safeguards visitor information, and ensures safe promo code distribution.

1. Information Collection & Use

What personal information does DBD Codes collect?

DBD Codes does not require user accounts, passwords, or payment credentials. Our service is completely free to access without registration.

How is technical log data processed?

We may process standard non-identifiable technical logs (IP address, browser type, device operating system) solely to optimize site performance and prevent automated abuse.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

Why do we use local browser preferences?

We use local browser storage strictly for functional preferences such as dark/light mode settings and community rating feedback.

How do third-party offer networks handle verification?

Third-party offer networks may use cookies to track offer completion for human verification purposes.

3. Third-Party Links & Outgoing Traffic

What happens when visiting external partner websites?

Our website contains links to third-party verification providers. Once you leave our domain, your activity is governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Contact & Questions

For questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@dbdcodes.com.
